# Five Poems

---

## 1. The Survey in the Maize Fields

Across the green rows of Webuye's land,
A clipboard passes from hand to hand.
What did you plant? What did you sell?
Each answer a story the numbers tell.

The maize stands tall in the afternoon heat,
And farmers reckon their harvest's feat—
Price per kilo, kilos per acre,
Each datum a truth the analyst will savor.

---

## 2. Roster

Five columns wide, a thousand rows deep,
A list of names the spreadsheet keeps.
LR15, LR16, LR17—
Three cohorts passing through the frame unseen.

Each row a person, each cell a fact,
A roster built and then stacked, restacked.
Behind the numbers: a face, a life,
A mother, a student, a husband, a wife.

---

## 3. Repayment

Month by month the ledger grows,
Vertical columns in measured rows.
What is owed and what has been paid—
The careful arithmetic of promises made.

No drama here, just decimal places,
Interest rates and loan-term spaces.
Yet underneath each tidy sum,
A family waiting for drought to be done.

---

## 4. Exploratory

Before the model, before the test,
Comes the honest, unhurried quest:
Plot a histogram, check the spread,
Find the outliers, the living, the dead.

The data does not yield its truth
To anyone who skips this proof—
The slow walk through a scatterplot
Reveals the structure others forgot.

---

## 5. Check-In

A flag is raised, a box is checked,
The field team's progress is tracked and wrecked
By rain and road and broken phone—
Yet the check-in script runs on, alone.

Extract the timestamp, parse the date,
Log who was early, log who was late.
The survey breathes in real-world time,
And every check-in is a kind of rhyme.
